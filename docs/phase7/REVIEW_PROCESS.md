# Phase 7 editorial review process

Established 2026-10-28. Applies to new notes, cards and original exam questions. The user reviews learning experience, not factual correctness item by item.

## Ownership

Author: researches and drafts. Independent reviewer: opens sources and challenges the proposed answer, including every distractor. Lead: resolves findings using evidence and approves or excludes. Record the actual reviewer/run, not an invented human expert identity. A different agent is an independent pass, not a guarantee of domain expertise. If no independent reviewer is available, material remains pending.

## Workflow and record

draft -> source-reviewed -> independently-reviewed -> approved

Exceptions: needs-revision, blocked-source, excluded. Only approved items count toward publishable totals. Answer/meaning/source-applicability changes invalidate approval for that revision.

For each item record:
- Stable ID, revision, concept ID, primary topic/domain and objective mapping.
- Author/reviewer identities and review date.
- Source URL and section, precise supported claim, applicable deployment/channel/feature status.
- Correct answer reasoning and why each incorrect option fails under the stated conditions.
- Duplicate candidates and disposition.
- Review findings, changes, evidence-based resolution and remaining uncertainties.
- Schema/reference/scoring check result and final status.

Source accessedAt means a page was opened. claimReviewedAt means the exact claim was examined. A landing page review must not populate claimReviewedAt for all linked units. Never update dates in bulk to imply re-verification.

## Mandatory quality gates

1. Evidence directly supports the answer. A title, search snippet, homepage or model agreement is insufficient.
2. The complete answer set is unambiguous under the stated requirements. Challenge the strongest alternative.
3. Deployment model, permissions, dependencies and temporal conditions needed to answer are explicit.
4. Explanation teaches the decision and accurately explains distractors; no unsupported absolute claims.
5. Single-answer has exactly one correct option. Multiple-answer states its selection count and uses exact-set scoring.
6. Distinctness comes from tested knowledge or materially different application, not swapped names/numbers.
7. Preview, licensing, regional, numeric-limit and telephony claims have specific current primary evidence.
8. Note and card wording do not convert personal annotations into verified source material.
9. IDs, references, options, revisions and objective mappings pass automated validation. Passing validation is not factual approval.

Unresolved material contradictions block publication. Rewrite to a supported claim or exclude; do not ask the learner to settle technical disputes. A blueprint objective establishes scope, not behavior or availability.

## Taxonomy and coverage checks

Every exam item gets one primary domain for weighting, additional objective links as needed, a tested-concept ID, and source-linked related notes. Study cards use controlled topic/feature/task tags and optional exam mappings. Share canonical content where the meaning is identical; keep distinct applications separate. Cards and exam questions are separate content types.

Track objective coverage through authored and approved IDs, not question count alone. Flag gaps, redundant prompts, predictable answer-position patterns, implausible distractors, answer leakage and memorization-only scenarios. Long prose must serve a decision, not inflate difficulty.

## Batch report template

- Batch ID, revision/date, topic/domain and intended learner outcome.
- Drafted / approved / needs-revision / blocked / excluded counts.
- Notes/cards/questions approved; coverage gained and remaining gaps.
- Three to five representative examples for user feedback.
- Independent-review findings and substantive corrections.
- Unresolved source conflicts and lead's recommended disposition.
- Validation results and limitations.
- Next batch and user review gate.

The user's approval concerns clarity, usefulness, difficulty and continuation. It does not substitute for the factual gates above.

## Maintenance

Before approving a batch, reopen sources for time-sensitive claims. Keep a dependency map from source/concept to notes/cards/questions so later product changes can flag affected material. Preserve historical question snapshots. Re-review changed content before publishing a new revision; never retroactively regrade saved attempts. Future matching/drag questions and supplied external answer keys require this same review process.

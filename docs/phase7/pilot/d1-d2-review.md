## Mapping, scope, and duplicate checks

D1 mappings match the official guide's workspace/embedded, solution/ALM/environment, and role-management objectives. D2 mappings match widget embedding, surveys, SDK extension, authentication, context variables, and automatic identification. These are narrow coverage contributions, not completion of the whole domains.

The Salesforce scenario explicitly provides provisioning prerequisites. Recovery uses the applicable channel-provisioning or routing-enablement path instead of asserting that both always apply. Customer chat and representative embedded widgets remain distinct.

The standard preconversation Contact phone mapping is the scope of ab250-d2-q02. A separate AI-agent handoff table on the identification page uses different terminology; the question does not generalize its answer to that workflow or to custom field configuration. The SDK scenario explicitly concerns anonymous chat. The consent question makes no legal compliance claim.

I compared D1/D2 concepts and question stems with the D3/D4 and D5/D6 pilot inventories. Workspace-host choice differs from session-template composition; context setup differs from voice-variable lifetime and runtime workspace parameters; migration associations differ from routing eligibility. No duplicate exam scenario was found. Cards intentionally reinforce concepts later applied in exam questions; they are separate content types, not duplicate selectable cards.
